Turn any PDF book into an audiobook you can press play on.

Drop in a book. Spoken Page pulls out the text, splits it into tracks by chapter, and reads it aloud with the voice you pick. It remembers where you stopped.

  • Chapters from the PDF outline or headings
  • Speed 0.75× to 2×
  • Sleep timer
  • Tap any sentence to jump there
